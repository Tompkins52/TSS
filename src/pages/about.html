About

We design, build, and integrate systems.

TSS is a veteran-owned systems developer and integrator. We publish our own brands of apps, and we build and connect complete systems for federal, state, and local government, businesses of every size, and nonprofits.

What we do

Our own brands. We publish apps under brands of our own, each a family with its own name, look, and rules: Upfront for Small Business for running a business and Pass Muster Training for passing a license or certification test, with more brands on the way, plus Painless Project Management and Witch Card. They run on the web today, with Google Play and the App Store next. See Our products.

Systems for our customers. We design, build, integrate, and run complete systems under the customer's name: the people, the paperwork, the data, and the apps around them, connected to what the organization already uses. IntelliSafe runs annual training, incident reports, and site inspections for public works crews. Phantom Soldier, built pro bono for a veterans nonprofit, turns veterans' stories into songs. See Customer products.

Who we are

TSS started with tools built on nights and weekends for a problem every public works crew knows: annual safety training kept in a spreadsheet and incident reports filed in a drawer. That work became IntelliSafe. The company was organized on October 21, 2026, to do this work properly: under a company name, with support behind it, and with apps published under our own organization accounts.

We are a systems company, not a software shop. Code is one part of a system; the rest is understanding how the work is actually done, who does it, what has to be on record, and what it has to connect to. Every system we deliver is supported by the people who built it, and our prices are published, not hidden behind a call.

Mission

Design, build, and integrate systems that work the way our customers work, publish brands of apps people can trust, and stand behind everything we deliver.

Vision

To be the systems partner that organizations of every size count on, from federal agencies and local governments to small businesses and nonprofits, and to publish app brands that people trust on sight.

Values

  1. Plain language

    If someone cannot understand it on a phone in a truck, it is not finished. Our apps, our documentation, and this website are written to be read, not decoded.

  2. Built around the work

    We start from how the work is actually done, then build or connect the system around it. People should not have to change how they work to fit the tool.

  3. Respect the data

    We collect the least we can, and the customer owns their records. Part 107 Prep and Billhead keep everything on the user's own device, and Witch Card never asks for a card number. IntelliSafe runs on the department's own server or in the browser, not ours.

  4. Plain prices

    The price is shown before you start. No contracts, no ads, and never a cut of your money; if an app ever charges by the month, you can cancel any time. For customer systems, the price on the quote is the price.

  5. Finish the job

    Support windows that are written down, training content with the date it was reviewed, and a written after-action review at the end of every engagement: what we planned, what happened, and what we would change.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a one-page charter. It names the sponsor and the lead, says what we are building and why, defines what done looks like, sets the budget, and sets the dates. Until that page is complete, there is no project. It is the same method we ship in Painless Project Management, because we built the tool for ourselves first.

While the work is under way you get a two-minute update every week: schedule, budget, and whether we need help from you. Red is not failure; hiding red is. When the work is delivered you get a written after-action review: what was planned, what actually happened, why, what to sustain, and what to improve.
