Brand

Name, logo, colors, type.

Everything a partner, a reporter, or a procurement office needs to refer to us correctly. The logo files are free to use when writing about the company or listing us as a vendor.

The name

  • Legal name: Tompkins Software Systems LLC. Use it in contracts, invoices, and vendor records.
  • Everyday name: Tompkins Software Systems on first mention, TSS after that.
  • Do not use "Tompkins Software," "Tompkins Systems," or "T.S.S."
  • Product names are written as shown on their pages: IntelliSafe, Painless Project Management, Part 107 Prep, Witch Card, Billhead, and Phantom Soldier. IntelliSafe is one word with a capital I and S, and the name belongs to IntelliSafe Systems LLC.
  • Product families are written Upfront for Small Business and Pass Muster Training on first mention, Upfront and Pass Muster after that. See below.

Product families

Our apps are published in families, and new families are added as the line grows. Each family is its own brand, with its own name, mark, colors, and typeface. Every app in a family keeps its own name, and its product page, its store listing, and the site footer say which family it belongs to. Tompkins Software Systems makes every one of them.

Logo

A brass T on an evergreen tile with a paper baseline. Use the lockup where there is room and the mark alone at small sizes. Do not recolor or stretch it.

Colors

Evergreen and brass on paper. Four colors do the work; lighter and darker steps of the green and brass handle buttons, links, and the footer, and those values are in the stylesheet.

  • Evergreen#12352b header bands, footer, buttons
  • Brass#d4a84b accent, never large areas
  • Paper#fbfbf8 page background
  • Ink#1a221e text

Typography

Source Serif 4

Headings and the wordmark, weight 600. An open-source serif with optical sizing, chosen because it reads as an institution rather than a startup.

Public Sans

Body text, interface, and labels, weights 400 to 700. The typeface of the U.S. Web Design System, so it is already familiar to anyone who reads government websites.

Both typefaces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License and are self-hosted on this site; no font requests go to a third party.

Voice

  • Plain, specific, and short. Say what the product does, not what it "empowers."
  • Numbers over adjectives: "twelve modules," not "comprehensive training."
  • No exclamation points. No em dashes. Sentences end with periods.
  • Status labels are literal: Available, In preparation, Internal. Never "coming soon" without a reason.
  • Write for a crew lead reading on a phone in a truck.
