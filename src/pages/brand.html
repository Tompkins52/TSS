Brand

Name, logo, colors, type.

Everything a customer, a reporter, or a procurement office needs to refer to us correctly. The logo and family mark files are free to use when writing about the company or listing us as a vendor.

The name

  • Legal name: Tompkins Software Systems LLC. Use it in contracts, invoices, and vendor records.
  • Everyday name: TSS, from the first mention. Spell out the legal name only where a document needs it.
  • Do not use "Tompkins Software," "Tompkins Systems," or "T.S.S."
  • Product names are written as shown on their pages: IntelliSafe, Part 107 Prep, Witch Card, Billhead, and Phantom Soldier. IntelliSafe is one word with a capital I and S. The product is owned and licensed by Tompkins Software Systems LLC and was built for IntelliSafe Systems LLC.
  • Product families are written Upfront for Small Business and Pass Muster Training on first mention, Upfront and Pass Muster after that. See below.

Product families

Our apps are published in families, and new families are added as the line grows. Each family is its own brand, with its own name, mark, colors, and typeface. Every app in a family keeps its own name, and its product page, the site footer, and its store listing once published say which family it belongs to. TSS makes every one of them.

Logo

The TS shield: a T and an S locked into a shield in blue, sky blue, and brushed silver, shaded so it reads as metal, beside TSS in blue. On dark backgrounds the blues brighten and TSS turns white. The flat version, in solid blue #004a88, sky #00a8e6, and silver #8a95a5, is for print, embroidery, and anything one-color. Use the lockup where there is room and the shield alone at small sizes. Do not recolor or stretch it.

Colors

Steel gray with the logo's two blues. Five colors do the work; lighter and darker steps handle buttons, links, and the footer, and those values are in the stylesheet. Green and amber appear only as status colors.

  • Blue#004a88 buttons, links, the wordmark
  • Sky#00a8e6 accent lines and highlights, never text on white
  • Slate#5f6b7a SOFTWARE SYSTEMS and quiet text
  • Paper#f7f8fa page background

Typography

Montserrat

Headings and the wordmark, weights 600 to 800. A geometric sans that matches the logo's lettering; the wordmark is set in capitals.

Public Sans

Body text, interface, and labels, weights 400 to 700. The typeface of the U.S. Web Design System, so it is already familiar to anyone who reads government websites.

Both typefaces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License and are self-hosted on this site; no font requests go to a third party.
