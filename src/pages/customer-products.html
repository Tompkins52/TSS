Phantom Soldier

Veteran stories, told as songs. A veteran writes a story down, a real person works through the words with them, and it comes back as a song released under the Phantom Soldier name, never theirs. We built phantomsoldier.org and keep it running: a page for every song and the story behind it, a story intake form, a Veterans Service Officer finder, and printable flyers.

The nonprofit side

It costs the veteran nothing, and everything the music earns is donated to veteran suicide prevention. Phantom Soldier runs on a nonprofit basis through Project Veteran Overwatch, which is pursuing 501(c)(3) status. Project Veteran Overwatch is separate from Tompkins Software Systems and owns the site and the music; we do the work at no charge and take nothing from it. It is a storytelling project, not medical or clinical care.
