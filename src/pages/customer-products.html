Phantom Soldier

Veteran stories, told as songs and released under the Phantom Soldier name, never the veteran's. We built phantomsoldier.org and the system behind it, and keep it running.

The nonprofit side

Project Veteran Overwatch offers it free to the veteran and puts everything the music earns toward veteran suicide prevention. PVO, which is pursuing 501(c)(3) status, is separate from us and owns the site and the music. We work at no charge. It is storytelling, not clinical care.
