Billhead

Invoices from your phone. Set up your company once, then make a simple or an advanced invoice by answering a few questions or by typing on the invoice itself. Projects billed in stages say which invoice this is, what was billed before, and what remains; payments turn invoices partly paid and paid; and the PDF goes out locked, with an Invoice ID on every page. Any invoice under $100 is free. It replaces TSS Invoice Creator.

For: contractors, tradespeople, freelancers, and small businesses, from a few invoices a month to a few hundred a year.

Witch Card

A phone app that tells you which credit card to use at the register. Open it at the store and it tells you where you are standing and which of your cards earns the most there, with the rest ranked under it and caps, rotating categories, and Visa-only stores worked out. A perks checklist links to each bank's activation page. It covers 229 cards from 25 issuers, reads a card with the camera, and has no ads, sponsors, or referral links.

For: anyone who carries more than one credit card and pays the statement in full.

Phantom Soldier

Veteran stories, told as songs. A project of Project Veteran Overwatch: a veteran tells a story, it comes back as a song released under one name, never theirs, and everything the music earns goes to veteran suicide prevention. We built and maintain the website at no charge, and PVO owns it.

How we decide what to build

A product gets on this list when three things are true: we needed it ourselves, other people or organizations have the same problem, and the software already out there is priced or designed for someone else. We would rather publish five tools that work than a roadmap of twenty. Pro bono work gets on the list when a veteran-serving organization needs something built and we can finish it.

Why families

Apps for the same people live together under one name and one set of rules, so once you know one, you know what to expect from the next. Every Upfront app puts its price on the tag and never asks for a subscription. Every Squared Away app runs on the same study engine. Each app keeps its own name and look, and its page says which family it belongs to.

What every product comes with

  • A product page with an honest status label, a piece of the product to try, and a dated changelog.
  • A support channel that reaches the people who wrote the code.
  • A privacy section that says, in plain terms, what data the product touches.
  • No required subscription. If a product needs hosting, you can host it yourself.
