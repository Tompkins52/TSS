How we decide what to build

When three things are true: we needed it ourselves, others have the same problem, and what is out there is priced or designed for someone else.

Why families

Apps for the same people share one name and one set of rules, so once you know one, you know what to expect from the next.

What every product comes with

  • An honest status label and a piece to try.
  • Support from the people who wrote the code.
  • A plain-language privacy section.
  • Plain prices, shown before you start.
