Part 107 Prep

A free study site and a mobile app for the FAA remote pilot certificate, the commercial drone license. Study modules, more than 200 original practice questions with explanations, timed exams, a daily goal, and spaced review. All progress stays on your own device.

For: anyone studying for the Part 107 knowledge test, including municipal drone programs.

Which Card

A phone app that tells you which credit card to use at the register. Open it at the store and it tells you where you are standing and which of your cards earns the most there, with the rest ranked under it and caps, rotating categories, and Visa-only stores worked out. A perks checklist links to each bank's activation page. It covers 228 cards from 25 issuers and has no ads, sponsors, or referral links.

For: anyone who carries more than one credit card and pays the statement in full.

Veteran stories, told as songs. A project of Project Veteran Overwatch: a veteran tells a story, it comes back as a song released under one name, never theirs, and everything the music earns goes to veteran suicide prevention. We built and maintain the website at no charge, and PVO owns it.

How we decide what to build

A product gets on this list when three things are true: we needed it ourselves, other people or organizations have the same problem, and the software already out there is priced or designed for someone else. We would rather publish four tools that work than a roadmap of twenty. Pro bono work gets on the list when a veteran-serving organization needs something built and we can finish it.

What every product comes with

  • A product page with an honest status label, a piece of the product to try, and a dated changelog.
  • A support channel that reaches the people who wrote the code.
  • A privacy section that says, in plain terms, what data the product touches.
  • No required subscription. If a product needs hosting, you can host it yourself.
