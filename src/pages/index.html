Billhead makes the invoice on your phone from a company profile you set up once: line items, sales tax, a discount or a deposit, and on an advanced invoice the PO, the job site, retainage, and progress billing (invoice 2 of 3). It keeps track of what went out and what came in, and the PDF goes out locked. Nothing is sent to us.

Free while in testing. Then 99 cents an invoice, or $1.99 for an advanced one, paid once. Packs never expire, and a year of unlimited invoices is $99 with no auto-renew. Never a percentage of what your customer pays you.
