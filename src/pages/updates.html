What has changed, newest first.

Company news and product releases in one list. Each product page carries its own changelog; this is the summary.

  1. October 17, 2026

    Company website published as a working draft

    This site goes up with the company's products, policies, and procurement information. Contact channels and the newsletter open once the company's registration is complete.

  2. October 15, 2026

    Tompkins Software Systems LLC organized in Illinois

    Articles of Organization filed with the Illinois Secretary of State. The company takes over publishing and support for the four products that had been built independently, and will hold the organization accounts on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

  3. October 2026

    Part 107 Prep 1.1: daily goals, streaks, spaced review, achievements

    The mobile app asks for a daily goal on first launch, keeps a forgiving streak with two automatic freezes, schedules missed questions for review on a spaced interval, adds a question of the day, saves an in-progress exam after every answer, and unlocks sixteen achievements tied to real progress. All of it stays on the phone. iOS and Android builds are being prepared for store submission.

  4. October 2026

    TOC: charter-gated planning and after-action reports for our own builds

    Our internal command-line tool now plans work as a task graph with a critical path, routes tasks to the right tools with a rule against self-review, enforces a budget cap, and writes a five-part after-action report. Internal only.

  5. October 2026

    Public Works Safety Training: browser edition and full platform

    Twelve modules covering OSHA's ten most cited standards plus Pole Top Rescue and Bucket Truck Rescue, ten-question quizzes, certificates, automatic annual reassignment, email reminders, and incident reporting with investigation records. The browser edition runs with nothing to install; the full platform deploys with Docker, on a Linux or Windows server, or on Render.

  6. October 2026

    Part 107 Prep study site launched

    Five study modules, the certification process step by step, 14 CFR Part 107 section by section, a printable preflight checklist, and a practice exam engine with more than 200 original questions.

  7. October 2026

    Painless Project Management public demo

    The one-page method for running a project, released as a standalone build with three example projects loaded.
