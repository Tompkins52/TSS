What it does

Anyone flying a drone for work needs a Part 107 certificate, and many prep apps charge a subscription. We wrote our own questions, checked them against FAA sources, and made it free.

  • Five study modulesRegulations, airspace and charts, weather, loading and performance, and operations: the areas the FAA tests.
  • More than 200 original questionsEach with an explanation and a reference. The practice sectional chart matches its questions.
  • Exams like the real one60 timed questions in the FAA's proportions, scored at 70 percent, saved after every answer.
  • A daily goal you pick5 to 30 questions a day. Flashcards count too.
  • Spaced reviewA miss comes back tomorrow, a right answer later and later. Two streak freezes cover missed days.
  • Nothing leaves your phoneNo account, no server, no analytics.
Accuracy and licensing

Accuracy

Content was reviewed against FAA sources in October 2026, and the content date is shown inside the app. Regulations change, so confirm anything you rely on for a real flight against the current 14 CFR Part 107 and FAA guidance. Corrections are welcome through the support channel. Part 107 Prep is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA.
