What it does

Municipal drone programs are growing: inspections, mapping, storm damage surveys, public safety. Every operator needs a Part 107 certificate, and the existing prep apps are known for typos, wrong answers, charts that do not match the question, and a subscription for all of it. We wrote our own question bank, reviewed it against FAA sources, dated it, and gave it away.

  • Five study modulesRegulations, airspace and sectional charts, weather, loading and performance, and operations, matching the knowledge areas the FAA tests. Plus the certification process step by step and 14 CFR Part 107 section by section.
  • More than 200 original questionsEvery question has an explanation and a reference. The practice sectional chart is fictional but drawn correctly, so chart questions actually match the chart.
  • Exams that work like the real oneA full 60-question timed exam in the FAA's proportions, a 20-question quick check, topic quizzes, scoring at 70 percent, a category breakdown, and review of every miss. An exam in progress is saved after every answer.
  • A daily goal you pickFive, ten, twenty, or thirty questions a day. Questions and flashcards both count, and finishing a module counts in full.
  • Spaced review and a forgiving streakA missed question comes back tomorrow; a correct one moves out 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, then 60 days. Two streak freezes cover missed days automatically. A lost streak simply restarts.
  • Nothing leaves your phoneNo account, no server, no analytics. All progress is stored on the device, and we never see it.
Accuracy and licensing

Accuracy

Content was reviewed against FAA sources in October 2026, and the content date is shown inside the app. Regulations change, so confirm anything you rely on for a real flight against the current 14 CFR Part 107 and FAA guidance.
