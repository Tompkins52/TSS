    What it does

    If you carry more than one credit card, you have probably used the wrong one at the gas pump. The rules that decide it are public but scattered: rate tables, spending caps, quarterly calendars, card networks a store will not take, and stores that do not count as groceries. Witch Card keeps those rules for you. Open it at the store and the answer is already on the screen.

    • It knows where you are standingWith location on, open the app and it names the store you are in and shows the best card there before you type a thing. Type the amount for dollars and cents. The store next door is one tap away, and you can always pick the store yourself.
    • The fine print, worked outSpending caps, rotating 5 percent categories and their activation dates, Prime and non-Prime rates, stores that take Visa only, and the superstores and warehouse clubs that do not count as groceries.
    • Every perk on one screenEach card's credits and perks in one list with an Activated switch. If a perk is off, its button opens the bank's own activation page, and a reminder says when something still needs activating.
    • The best card you do not haveAt each store, the card from the full list that would earn the most there after its annual fee, at the number of visits you set, and how many visits it takes to cover the fee. Nobody pays to be listed.
    • 228 cards, checked every dayCards from 25 issuers and more than 200 stores by name, built from each issuer's own pages. Every source page is checked for changes daily, and a change is reviewed before the card data changes. Missing a card? Add it with the rates that matter to you.
    • Nothing to sell youNo ads, no sponsors, no referral links, and no account. You pick cards by name, the app never asks for a card number, and your wallet stays on your phone.
    How the answers are figured, and who it is for

    How the answers are figured

    With location on, the app checks the businesses within about 120 meters each time it opens and picks the closest one it recognizes, by name or by type. A place you have been in the last 30 days is answered straight from your phone, with no lookup. Each purchase is matched to the store's category and to any rule a card has for that store by name. Points and miles are counted in cents: at their cash value when cash back is your priority, or at transfer-partner rates when points and miles are. If you are working toward a sign-up bonus, the card with the unfinished spending goal goes first. The number shown is what that purchase earns under the card's published terms. Tap I used it and the purchase counts toward that card's spending caps.

    Who it is for

    People who pay the statement in full every month, so rewards are the only reason to pick one card over another. If you carry a balance, the interest costs more than any card gives back.

    Independence

    Witch Card has no affiliate or referral agreement with any bank, and nobody can pay for a place in its answers. The card pictures are original drawings made from each card's color, because the real designs are trademarked. Sign-up bonuses are shown as the banks publish them, with no referral links. Witch Card is not affiliated with or endorsed by any card issuer or card network. The witch is for fun; every answer is arithmetic on your own cards.

    Put it on your home screen

    On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. It opens full screen like any other app, and once it has loaded, the answers work without a signal.
