    What it does

    If you carry more than one credit card, you have probably used the wrong one at the gas pump. The rules that decide it are public but scattered: rate tables, spending caps, quarterly calendars, card networks a store will not take, and stores that do not count as groceries. Witch Card keeps those rules for you. Open it at the store and the answer is already on the screen.

    • It knows where you are standingWith location on, open the app and it names the store you are in and shows the best card there, with every other card you carry ranked under it, on one screen with nothing to type. When it is not sure, it offers the stores near you to tap. A place the map does not know, you name once and it remembers the spot.
    • The fine print, worked outSpending caps, rotating 5 percent categories and their activation dates, Prime and non-Prime rates, stores that take Visa only, and the superstores and warehouse clubs that do not count as groceries.
    • Every perk on one screenEach card's credits and perks in one list with an Activated switch. If a perk is off, its button opens the bank's own activation page, and a reminder says when something still needs activating. A card with rotating 5 percent categories shows this quarter's categories wherever it sits, with a check once you have activated them.
    • How your wallet doesCompare puts each kind of place on a scale from weak to great: a plain 1 percent card at one end, the best card anyone can get at the other, and a dot where your best card sits. When one of your cards pays extra at one company, like 5x at Lyft, the row says so with an asterisk. Tap a row for the best cards there, yours marked; a card you already hold that ties for the top is listed first, so it never suggests a card you do not need.
    • Sign-up offers for your goalThe banks' current welcome offers, ranked by what they are worth to you: cash if cash back is your goal, points at their travel value if that is. Keep it to one bank or a few when you want points that pool together. No referral links; the offer opens the bank's own page.
    • 229 cards, kept currentCards from 25 issuers and more than 200 stores by name, built from each issuer's own pages. Source pages are checked for changes on a schedule, and a change is reviewed before the card data changes. Add a card by name, or hold it up to the camera: the app reads the name printed on it, on the phone, letters only and never the number. Missing a card? Add it with the rates that matter to you.
    • Nothing to sell youNo ads, no sponsors, no referral links, and no account. You pick cards by name, the app never asks for a card number, and your wallet stays on your phone.
    How the answers are figured, and who it is for

    How the answers are figured

    With location on, the app checks the businesses around you each time it opens and names the one you are standing in when it is sure (inside its outline on the map, or within about 100 feet); otherwise it offers the nearest few to tap. A place you have been in the last 30 days is answered straight from your phone, with no lookup. Each purchase is matched to the store's category and to any rule a card has for that store by name. Points and miles are counted in cents: at their cash value when cash back is your priority, or at transfer-partner rates when points and miles are. If you are working toward a sign-up bonus, the card with the unfinished spending goal goes first. The rate shown is what the card earns there under its published terms. Travel counts flights and hotels booked directly, and booking through a bank's own travel site is its own kind of place, so a card that pays 8x through Chase Travel and 4x direct shows both.

    Who it is for

    People who pay the statement in full every month, so rewards are the only reason to pick one card over another. If you carry a balance, the interest costs more than any card gives back.

    Independence

    Witch Card has no affiliate or referral agreement with any bank, and nobody can pay for a place in its answers. The card pictures are original drawings made from each card's color, because the real designs are trademarked. Sign-up bonuses are shown as the banks publish them, with no referral links. Witch Card is not affiliated with or endorsed by any card issuer or card network. Store lookups use OpenStreetMap data, copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License. The witch is for fun; every answer is arithmetic on your own cards.

    Put it on your home screen

    On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. It opens full screen like any other app, and once it has loaded, the answers work without a signal.
