03 / What it does

What it does

Training, quizzes, certificates, reminders, incident investigations, and inspections in one place a small department can actually run, in a browser with nothing to install or on a server the department controls.

  • Nineteen training modulesOSHA's ten most cited standards, pole top and bucket truck rescue, bloodborne pathogens, fire safety, electrical safety, confined spaces, ergonomics, workplace violence, and incident investigation for supervisors.
  • A quiz and a certificate for eachTen questions, 80 percent to pass, retakes allowed, every attempt recorded, and a printable certificate when it is done.
  • Reminders that reach supervisionEmail at 30 days, at 7 days, and every 7 days once overdue, with a copy to the supervisor each time. Next year's assignment creates itself.
  • An eight-section incident reportIncident, vehicle and equipment, injury, description, principal and eyewitness statements, facts and analysis, and a four-signature block. Report numbers carry the date and type, such as 10232026PI-01.
  • Statements signed by the people who gave themEach witness sees only their own statement page and signs it on the investigator's device, through an emailed link, or on paper. Diagrams for the vehicle, the scene, and the body replace paragraphs.
  • Inspections and an annual auditConstruction (136 items), general industry (137 items), and a 69-item annual safety program audit, each item citing its OSHA reference. Findings get a responsible party and a target date.
Everything it does, in detail

Assignments that keep themselves current

When an employee passes, next year's assignment is created automatically. Ten modules form a required new hire track, assigned in one step when an employee is added, and the safety manager can assign any module to any employee. Supervisors are told when their people finish a module.

An investigation that has to be finished

Analysis of the facts, contributing causes, direct causes, root cause, and the contributing factor type: behavioral, engineered, or environmental. Corrective actions carry a responsible party, a target date, and a verification date, with the policies and regulations that apply. A report cannot be closed until the causes are recorded.

Records in one place

The full incident report prints to paper or PDF with its diagrams, statements, and signatures. Training records and inspections export to Excel. In the full platform, employees see what they reported, supervisors see their division and team, and safety administrators see everything.

Keeping the content current

The module list follows OSHA's annual list of most frequently cited standards. The ranking lives in one data file and is updated once a year. Each module and each inspection checklist is a plain text file an administrator can edit and reload without a developer.
