Billhead

Invoices from your phone. Set up your company once, then make a simple or an advanced invoice by answering a few questions or by typing on the invoice itself. Jobs billed in stages keep their own running totals, payments turn invoices partly paid and paid, the PDF goes out locked, and one tap fills in the email to your customer.

The price: any invoice under $100 is free. Above that, 99 cents for a simple invoice or $1.99 for an advanced one, paid once; packs that never expire; or $99 for a year with no auto-renew. No subscription, and never a percentage of what your customer pays you.

The rules every Upfront app keeps

They are the reason the family exists. An app that cannot keep them does not get the name.

  1. Plain prices

    What a thing costs is shown before you start, next to the button that does it. No hidden fees, no setup charges, and no pricing page to decode.

  2. No contracts

    Nothing to sign and nothing that locks you in. If an app ever charges by the month, you can cancel any time and the charges stop. Billhead has no subscription at all: pay per invoice, buy a pack that never expires, or pay once for a year.

  3. Never a cut of your money

    We charge for the tool, never a percentage of what your customers pay you.

  4. Your records are yours

    Kept on your own phone or computer wherever the job allows, ready to export whenever you like, and never sold. Billhead needs no account and keeps everything on your device.

  5. No ads

    We make money from the tool, never from your attention or your data.

  6. Works where you work

    On a phone at the job and on a computer at the desk.
