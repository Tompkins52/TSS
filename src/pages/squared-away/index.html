How every Squared Away app works

The same study engine under every test. What changes is the content and the tools a test needs, like the sectional chart for Part 107.

  1. A goal you pick

    5, 10, 20, or 30 a day, and questions and flashcards both count. A streak with two freezes, so one missed day does not undo a week.

  2. Answer, do not reread

    Every module ends in a quiz and a set of flashcards, because pulling an answer out of memory is what makes it stay.

  3. What you miss comes back

    A miss is due again tomorrow. A right answer moves out to 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, and then 60 days.

  4. Topics mixed like the test

    Quick checks and full exams mix every area of the test. A weak-spots mode leans on what you have missed.

  5. Every answer explained

    Original questions, each with an explanation and the rule or source it comes from.

  6. Your progress stays with you

    No account, no ads, no tracking. An exam in progress is saved after every answer, so a phone call never costs you a test.

Why it is built this way. Answering questions beats rereading, and spaced practice beats cramming. Both are among the best-replicated findings in learning research, so every Squared Away app is built around them.
