The Work Order

One email a month from Tompkins Software Systems: what shipped, what we learned building it, and what is next. Written by the people who write the software.

Helps us decide what to write about. We never use it for anything else.

You will get a confirmation email first. Every issue has an unsubscribe link, and it works on the first click.

What is in an issue

  • Releases and changelogs for every product
  • One short lesson from the month's work
  • Changes to the OSHA or FAA material our products depend on
  • What we are building next, and when

What is not

  • No marketing sequences or "drip" campaigns
  • No open or click tracking
  • No sharing your address with anyone
  • Never more than one issue a month
