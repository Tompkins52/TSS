How every Pass Muster app works

The same study engine under every test.

  1. A goal you pick

    5 to 30 a day. Two streak freezes, so one missed day does not undo a week.

  2. Answer, do not reread

    Every module ends in a quiz and flashcards. Recall is what makes it stay.

  3. What you miss comes back

    A miss is due tomorrow. A right answer waits longer each time.

  4. Topics mixed like the test

    Practice exams mix every area, the way the real test does.

  5. Every answer explained

    Original questions, each with an explanation and its source.

  6. Your progress stays with you

    No account, no ads, no tracking.
